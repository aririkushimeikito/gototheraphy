Privacy Policy

What we hold, why, and how to ask about it.

Last updated: date required

Legal review required — do not publish

Gototherapy holds registration data, insurance documents, identity documents and supervisor contact details for professional members, which makes this policy required rather than optional. The structure below follows what a UK GDPR privacy notice needs to say. Every placeholder needs the real position, reviewed by somebody qualified to do so.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Content requiredLegal entity, address, ICO registration number, and a contact email for data matters.

2. If you are looking for a therapist

Gototherapy is built so that you can use it without telling us anything about yourself. There is no enquiry form and no account for visitors. Searches are not stored against you. What you type into the search box is used to filter the page and is not sent to Gototherapy.

When you contact a professional, you contact them directly. What you tell them is between you and them and is covered by their own confidentiality and privacy obligations, not by this policy.

Content requiredServer logs (IP address, user agent) and how long they are kept. Whether any analytics tool is used, and what it collects.

3. If you are a professional member

To check and list you, Gototherapy holds: your name and contact details; your professional role and register entry, including registration number and expiry date; your insurance certificate and its expiry date; your supervisor's name and contact details, and their written confirmation; a photo identity document, viewed for the purpose of matching you to your register entry; your profile text, fees, availability and (if supplied) your photograph; your subscription status and a Stripe customer reference. Card details are never held by Gototherapy.

Content requiredConfirm the identity document retention position (the join page states the image is deleted after the check) and where documents are stored.

4. Why we are allowed to use it

Content requiredLawful bases per purpose: contract (membership), legitimate interests (verification, fraud prevention), consent (marketing emails), legal obligation (accounting records).

5. Who we share it with

Content requiredStripe (payments), email provider, hosting provider, professional bodies when checking a register entry, your supervisor when confirming the arrangement. International transfers, if any.

6. How long we keep it

Content requiredRetention periods per category, including after membership ends.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data Gototherapy holds about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to some uses, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Content requiredHow to make a request and how long a response takes.

8. Cookies and analytics

This site sets a session cookie for members who log in. It does not set advertising cookies.

Content requiredFull cookie list once the backend and any analytics are chosen. If a consent banner is legally required for the chosen tools, add it; do not add one otherwise.

9. Security

Content requiredEncryption in transit and at rest, access controls on verification documents, and the breach notification process.

10. Contact and complaints

See the complaints page for how to raise a concern about Gototherapy or about a listed professional.
