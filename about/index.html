About

Every therapist here has been checked by a person.

Gototherapy is a UK directory of therapists, supervisors, coaches and trainers. It exists because most directories let anyone list, and most people cannot tell a register entry from a certificate. Here, a person checks each member's registration, insurance and supervision, rechecks them every year, and prints what was checked on the profile. There are no reviews or ratings.

Why the checks, and why they are printed

Looking for a therapist is hard enough without having to work out whether the person you are reading about is who they say they are. A counsellor is checked against a register that can strike them off. A coach, in the UK, has no register at all. A single “verified” badge across both would lend the credibility of the regulated professions to the unregulated ones, so Gototherapy does not use one. The badge says which checks were done for that role, and where there is no register, the profile says so.

We print the checks rather than asking you to trust us, because “trusted” is a claim and “checked on this date against this register” is a fact you can look at.

Why there are no reviews

Reviews of therapy are not like reviews of a restaurant. They reward the therapist who was pleasant over the one who was useful, they cannot be verified without breaching confidentiality, and they push people to write about their own mental health in a public place. Gototherapy has no review or rating fields anywhere, by design.

Why there is no enquiry form

A message about somebody's mental health sitting in a directory's database is special category data that did not need to exist. You contact therapists directly, and what you say to them stays between you.

What Gototherapy does and does not do

  • Does

    Check each member's register entry, insurance certificate, supervision arrangement and identity, per role, and repeat the checks annually.

  • Does

    Print what was checked, when, and what the badge for that role means.

  • Does

    Compute counts, fee ranges and availability from live member data rather than typing numbers that go stale.

  • Does not

    Provide therapy, recommend individual therapists, or rank anyone by popularity or payment.

  • Does not

    Carry reviews, ratings, testimonials or enquiry forms.

  • Does not

    Act as an emergency service. If you need help now, see urgent support.
