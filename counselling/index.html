Counselling by town and issue

Where you are, and what you need.

These pages list verified counsellors and psychotherapists by town, by issue, and by town and issue together. A town page describes what looking for a therapist there is actually like. An issue page describes what that thing tends to feel like and what a first session about it tends to cover, and carries a named clinical reviewer. Pages only appear in search engines once there are enough verified therapists to be useful.
