Application received

Thank you. A person will take it from here.

Your application is in the queue for verification. Nothing is paid until your checks are complete and you have been accepted.

What happens next

  1. 01

    Email confirmation

    A confirmation email is sent to the address you gave. If it has not arrived, check your junk folder, then get in touch.

  2. 02

    The checks

    A person checks your register entry against the register itself, looks at your insurance certificate, contacts your supervisor, and matches your identity to your register entry.

  3. 03

    Outcome by email

    You are told which checks passed, and asked for anything still needed. If everything is complete, you are invited to set up membership.

  4. 04

    Your profile goes live

    Once membership is active, your profile is published with the badge for your role. You manage it from your account.

Timeframe not statedHow long verification takes is supplied by the backend from real queue data, never typed here. Do not add an estimate to this page.
