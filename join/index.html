Join as a professional

Apply in six sections. A person checks the rest.

Joining Gototherapy means telling us who you are, what role you hold, and where we can check it: your professional register entry, your insurance, your supervision and your identity. A person does the checking and rechecks every year. Nobody pays before acceptance, and nothing you enter is stored in your browser.

Fields marked *asterisk are required.

About you

The name here is the one on your register entry. You can show a different name on your profile later.

We send the confirmation and the outcome of your checks here.

Only used if we need to check something about your application.

At least 12 characters.

Your professional role

Choose every role you hold. Holding two roles means meeting both standards: a therapist who is also a coach still has their register checked.

Roles*

Printed on your profile

Coaching and training provision are not regulated professions in the UK. Your profile will say: “This is not a regulated profession in the UK, so there is no register to check.” It is styled as a fact, not a warning.

For example “Counsellor” or “Psychotherapist and supervisor”. Please do not add qualifications we cannot check.

Where we can check you

A person checks each of these. Documents are uploaded to private storage, never to your browser. This prototype does not upload anything.

Identity

We match the person applying to the person on the register. A passport or driving licence is enough. We do not keep the image after the check.

Your practice

Where and how you work. Fees and availability can be changed in your account at any time; counts and ranges on the site are computed from what members enter, never typed.

Used to place you on town pages. Never shown in full.

Session format*

Optional now. If left blank your profile says fees are available on request.

A sentence is fine. You set exact weekly hours in your account after acceptance.

Your profile, in your words

Write as though you are answering somebody who has been putting off this email for months. Four questions, 40 to 80 words each, plain English, first person. Please do not list modalities without explaining them, and do not promise outcomes. We will not publish claims about results.

Optional now, but a profile is only indexed by search engines once it has a real photograph of you. Never a stock image. Until then, your initials are shown.

Check it, then send it

Anything marked missing will stop the application. Use Edit to go back; nothing you entered is lost.

Consent and terms

Nothing is paid now

Membership is only set up after acceptance. You will receive an email when your checks are complete. What membership includes.

Backend required: this form posts to POST /api/applications. In the prototype nothing is sent or stored.

Have these to hand

  • Your registration number and renewal date
  • Your insurance certificate
  • Your supervisor’s name and email
  • Photo ID
  • About 20 minutes

What happens next

A person checks each item, contacts your supervisor, and emails you the outcome. Your profile is published only when every check for your role is complete.
