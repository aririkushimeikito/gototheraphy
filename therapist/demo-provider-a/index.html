How I work, in plain English

Person-centred

You lead. The therapist listens closely, does not direct, and works on the idea that you are the best judge of what you need to talk about.

Cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT)

Structured and practical. Looks at the link between what you think, how you feel and what you do, usually with tasks between sessions.
