Stories

Written for somebody who has been putting this off.

Stories are short, plain pieces about starting therapy in the UK: what a first session is actually like, how much it costs and how to ask, whether online works, and how to tell if it is doing anything. Each one opens with a direct answer you can take away on its own. None of them is a testimonial, and none is a substitute for talking to a professional.

  • Starting · 6 min read

    What happens in a first therapy session?

    What the first hour is actually for, what a therapist is likely to ask, and why you do not have to decide anything in the room.

  • Money · 6 min read

    How much does therapy cost in the UK?

    How private fees are set, what affects them, what is free on the NHS, and how to ask about cost without embarrassment.

  • Online · 5 min read

    Can I have therapy online?

    Who online counselling suits, what changes when the room is a screen, and how to set it up so it works.

  • Keeping going · 6 min read

    How do I know if therapy is working?

    It rarely feels like progress from the inside. What to look for, what to say to your therapist, and when to stop.
