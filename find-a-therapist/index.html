What does verified mean on this page?

Verified means a person at Gototherapy has checked the therapist's entry on their professional register, seen a current insurance certificate, confirmed their supervision arrangement with the supervisor, and matched their identity to the register entry. It is repeated every year. It is not a recommendation and it says nothing about whether a therapist is right for you.

Roles are checked differently. A coach in the UK has no register, so the coach badge says what was checked and the profile says plainly that there is no register. How we verify, role by role.
