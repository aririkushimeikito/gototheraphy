How we verify

What was checked, by whom, and when.

Gototherapy verifies each professional by having a person check their entry on their professional register, see a current insurance certificate, confirm their supervision arrangement with the supervisor in writing, and match their identity to the register entry. The checks depend on the role, are repeated every year, and are printed on the profile. Roles with no register say so in plain words.

How does Gototherapy verify a therapist?

Four checks, done by a person, in this order.

  1. 01

    Register

    We look the person up on the register itself, not on a certificate they send us. We record the registration number and the expiry or renewal date.

  2. 02

    Insurance

    We see a current professional indemnity certificate and record when it expires.

  3. 03

    Supervision

    The therapist tells us who supervises their work. We contact the supervisor, who confirms the arrangement in writing. Nothing about clients is asked.

  4. 04

    Identity

    We match the person applying to the person on the register using photo identification. The image is deleted after the check.

Per role

The badge is conditional, and this is why.

A counsellor is checked against a register that can strike them off. A coach has no register: anybody may use the title tomorrow morning. One badge across both would transfer the credibility of the regulated professions onto the unregulated ones. So the badge says what was checked for that role, and nothing more.

RoleChecks requiredThe badge says
TherapistRegister, expiry, insurance, supervision, identityRegister, insurance and supervision checked
SupervisorRegister, expiry, insurance, identity, supervision trainingRegister, insurance and supervision training checked
Coach or wellbeing practitionerInsurance, identityInsurance and identity checked, plus the caveat
TrainerRegister, expiry, insurance, identityRegister and insurance checked
Training providerInsurance, identity, course accreditationCourse accreditation and insurance checked, plus the caveat

“This is not a regulated profession in the UK, so there is no register to check.”

Printed on every unregulated profile. Styled as a fact, never as a warning against the practitioner.

Holding two roles means meeting both standards. A therapist who is also a coach does not get to drop the register check.

What does therapist verification mean, and what does it not mean?

Verification means the checks above were completed on a stated date. It means the person is who they say they are, holds the registration they claim, is insured, and is supervised. It is printed with the date it was done and the date the next annual recheck is due.

It does not mean Gototherapy recommends the person, that they are right for you, or that anyone has assessed the quality of their work. Nobody can check that from documents, and we do not claim to.

What happens every year?

Every check is repeated. We track the expiry dates of registrations and insurance policies and email members before they lapse. If a document expires and is not renewed, the profile is hidden until it is. The verification history on a member's account shows each check and recheck.

What if a register entry changes?

Members agree to tell us if their registration, insurance or supervision changes. If a register entry is suspended or removed, or a check cannot be completed, the badge is removed and the profile is hidden. How to tell us about a concern.
