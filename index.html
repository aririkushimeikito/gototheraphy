What this is

A directory where somebody has done the checking.

Gototherapy lists UK counsellors, psychotherapists, supervisors, coaches and trainers. Before anyone appears, a person checks their professional register entry, their insurance and their supervision arrangement, and rechecks them every year.

You can search by town, by what you want help with, or for online sessions only. You contact the therapist directly. We do not sit in the middle, we do not take a message about your mental health, and we do not carry reviews. What you read on a profile is what the therapist wrote and what we checked.

Why Gototherapy exists

Verification

What was checked, not who is trusted.

A badge here says exactly which checks were done for that role. A counsellor is checked against a register that can strike them off. A coach has no register, and the profile says so in plain words.

  • Professional register

    The entry is checked against the register itself, not a certificate. The expiry date is recorded and rechecked.

  • Insurance

    A current professional indemnity certificate is seen, and its expiry date is tracked.

  • Supervision

    Therapists confirm who supervises their work, and the supervisor confirms it in writing.

  • Identity

    The person applying is matched to the person on the register. Every year, all of it is checked again.

“This is not a regulated profession in the UK, so there is no register to check.”

Printed on every coaching and training-provider profile. Styled as a fact, not a warning.

How it works

Four steps, and none of them is a form about you.

  1. 01

    Search

    By town, by what you want help with, or online only. Filters narrow the list; nothing ranks it.

  2. 02

    Read the profile

    Four questions, answered by the therapist in their own words, and a list of exactly what we checked.

  3. 03

    Get in touch directly

    You contact the therapist, not us. Many offer a short call first. You are allowed to ask about cost before anything else.

  4. 04

    Decide after the first session

    A first session is a conversation, not a commitment. You are allowed to not come back.

Plain answers

Questions people ask before they start.

How do I find a therapist in the UK?

Search a directory where the therapist's registration has been checked, read a few profiles, and contact one or two directly. Ask about cost and availability in the first message. Most therapists offer a short introductory call, and the first session is a conversation rather than a commitment.

The full step-by-step guide.

How does Gototherapy verify therapists?

A person checks each applicant's entry on their professional register, sees a current insurance certificate, confirms their supervision arrangement with the supervisor, and matches their identity to the register entry. The checks differ by role and are repeated every year. What was checked is printed on the profile.

How we verify, role by role.

What happens when I contact a therapist?

You write to or call the therapist directly; Gototherapy does not take or store your message. They will usually reply with their availability and fee, and many offer a short call before a first session so you can hear how they work. You are allowed to contact more than one person.

Can I have therapy online?

Yes. Many therapists listed here work by video or phone, and the verification is the same as for in-person work. Filter any search to online only. Online sessions suit people who cannot travel, have unpredictable hours, or find it easier to talk from home.

More about online counselling.

What if I need help right now?

Gototherapy is a directory, not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999. For other urgent support, the urgent support page lists where to turn.
