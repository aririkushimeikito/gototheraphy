For professionals

One membership. The checks are the product.

Gototherapy membership is a subscription for counsellors, psychotherapists, supervisors, coaches, trainers and training providers. It includes a person checking your registration, insurance, supervision and identity, an annual recheck, and a public profile in town, issue and online searches. Nobody pays before their checks are complete and they have been accepted. Featured placement, where offered, is always labelled and never changes verification.

What every membership includes

The checks are the same whether you pay monthly or annually, and they do not change with featured placement. A person checks your entry on your professional register, sees a current insurance certificate, contacts your supervisor to confirm the arrangement, and matches your identity to the register entry. Supervisors also show their supervision training. Coaches and training providers are checked for insurance and identity, and their profiles say plainly that there is no register.

Every check is repeated once a year, and we email you before a document expires. If a check lapses and is not renewed, your profile is hidden until it is.

There are no reviews or ratings on Gototherapy, by design. Your listing is never ranked by popularity or by how much you pay.

Questions professionals ask

When do I pay?

After your checks are complete and you have been accepted. You receive an email with a link to set up membership through Stripe Checkout. Your profile is published when the first payment completes.

What happens if a payment fails?

Stripe retries the card and emails you. Your account shows the subscription as past due with a button to update your card. The exact grace period before a profile is hidden is set out in the cancellation and refunds policy.

Can I cancel?

Yes, from your account at any time. Your profile stays listed until the end of the period you have paid for. Refund terms are in the cancellation and refunds policy.

Does featured placement mean more verified?

No. Featured placement, when it is offered, is a labelled position on a search page. It is always marked as paid to the person searching, and it has no effect on which checks were done or what your badge says.

Do referral or commission fees apply?

No. Membership is a flat subscription. Gototherapy takes nothing per enquiry or per session.
